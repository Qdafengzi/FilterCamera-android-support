# FilterCamera Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026
Developer: Qdafengzi
Contact: [qdafengzi@gmail.com](mailto:qdafengzi@gmail.com)

## 1. Overview

FilterCamera is a local-first camera app. This policy explains how the app accesses, processes, stores and shares information. FilterCamera has no user accounts, does not sell personal information, and does not include developer-operated advertising services.

## 2. Information accessed or processed

To provide photography, video recording, live filters, beauty effects, portrait segmentation and gallery access, the app may access or process:

- Camera frames, captured photos and videos, and media you allow the app to read;
- Microphone audio only while recording video with sound after permission is granted;
- Face positions, contours and subject masks for beauty effects, reshaping, portrait blur and focus feedback;
- Filter parameters, camera settings, language and interface preferences;
- A recent system-provided location only after you enable Save location and grant permission. It is written to new photo Exif metadata;
- Device and app information, per-installation identifiers, performance metrics, API configuration, input and output sizes, feature events and error codes that Google ML Kit may collect for diagnostics and usage analytics.

Photos, video frames, face imagery, contours and segmentation images are processed on the device and are not uploaded to a developer server during normal camera use.

## 3. Permissions

- **Camera:** preview, photography, video and camera capability detection.
- **Microphone:** video sound; requested when the related feature is used.
- **Photos and videos:** recent-media preview, gallery access and reading media you authorize.
- **Location and media location:** optionally write a location to a new photo or read location metadata from authorized media. Save location is off by default.
- **Internet and network state:** may be merged by components such as ML Kit for component delivery, diagnostics and usage analytics; not used to upload your photos or videos to a developer server.
- **Vibration:** shutter feedback.

You may deny optional permissions or withdraw them in Android settings. Features that depend on a denied permission may be unavailable.

## 4. Local processing and storage

Rendering, filters, beauty processing, face detection, subject segmentation and photo processing primarily run on your device. Photos, videos and optional RAW DNG files are saved to the device media library. Temporary files are kept in app cache and deleted after processing or later removed by Android. Settings and filter preferences remain in private app storage until you clear app data or uninstall the app. Android backup or device migration may transfer some preferences according to your system and account settings.

## 5. Third-party components

FilterCamera uses Android Jetpack, CameraX and Google ML Kit. Google states that ML Kit diagnostic data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS and is not transferred as data sharing to third parties. Google's processing is governed by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Media you intentionally share through Android is handled by the app or service you select under its policy.

## 6. Retention and deletion

The developer does not operate user accounts or store your photos, videos or location on a developer server. Delete captured media from your device, clear cache or app data in Android settings, or uninstall the app to remove private local data. Google controls retention of ML Kit diagnostic data under its policies. Support information you voluntarily email is used to respond and troubleshoot, then deleted when no longer needed.

## 7. Security, children and changes

The app uses Android permissions, private app storage and system media storage. Third-party diagnostic data is transmitted using HTTPS. FilterCamera is not designed specifically for children under 13. This policy may be updated when features or data practices change, and the effective date will be revised.
